## Native streaming posters (2026-10-09)

All 15 recognized providers now use locally composed posters wherever their
collection cards appear. Dark, subtle brand-color gradient backgrounds are drawn
at display resolution, with centered logos in a consistent safe area. Fourteen
logos are vector SVGs decoded at twice their displayed dimensions for focus
scaling; AMC+ uses the bundled 887 × 460 transparent source. No server thumbnail
or compressed collection poster is enlarged for these cards. Unrecognized
collections retain normal artwork behavior. Existing focus border, glow, scale,
card dimensions and overlays are preserved. Provider-header artwork is unchanged.
