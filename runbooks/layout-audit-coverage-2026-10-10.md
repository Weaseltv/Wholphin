They cover unavailable server libraries and conditional states; they are not claimed as real server/device reproductions.

Hidden legacy Requests browse pages and nonexistent request-status menus are recorded as unavailable, not claimed as tested. Private screenshots, account databases and authentication data remain outside the repository.

For shared layout rules, changes and final build results, see [the audit report](layout-audit-2026-10-09.md).
