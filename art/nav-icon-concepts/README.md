# WeaselFin TV Navigation Icon Concepts

These sheets are selection mockups only. Production assets will be redrawn as Android vector drawable XML after a concept family is selected.

## Sheet Order

All three sheets use the same row-major order:

| Row | Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Search | Home | Favorites | Discover / Requests |
| 2 | Movies | TV Shows | Stand Up Comedy | UFC |
| 3 | Boxing | 4K Movies (LAN) | 4K TV Shows (LAN) | Settings |

## Concept Families

- **Set A - Cinematic Solid:** familiar media silhouettes, rounded construction, and maximum immediate recognition.
- **Set B - Compact Pictograms:** geometric, modular forms with the strongest separation between ordinary and LAN libraries.
- **Set C - Bold Emblems:** angular broadcast and sports-network character with more energetic silhouettes.

## Final Production Contract

- Android vector drawable XML, not SVG or PNG.
- `android:viewportWidth="960"` and `android:viewportHeight="960"`.
- `android:width="24dp"` and `android:height="24dp"`.
- Opaque `android:fillColor="#FFFFFF"` only.
- Filled paths only; no strokes, gradients, opacity, clip paths, or transformed groups.
- Artwork kept within the central 85 percent safe area and normalized for consistent 24dp optical weight.

The final kit will use the requested `ic_nav_*.xml` filenames and include a real-size readability proof.
